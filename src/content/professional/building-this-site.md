title: Building sidfromthesideline
date: 2026-10-24
summary: Why I started a personal archive, and how it is put together.
---

This site is my personal archive: a place to keep track of where I have been,
what I have worked on, and how I have tried to give back.

It is built with [Astro](https://astro.build), with each section (travel,
professional, outreach) stored as plain Markdown files in the repo. Every push
to `main` deploys automatically.
